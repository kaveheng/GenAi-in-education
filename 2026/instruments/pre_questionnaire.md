# Pre-seminar questionnaire

Administered individually through an online form (link or QR code) immediately **before** the chatbot interaction (after the chatbot asked the group to complete it). Title of the form: *AI Literature Seminar 2026 – Pre-Seminar Questionnaire*.

## Consent item

> I have received information about the research study connected to this seminar. I understand that participation is voluntary and that my decision will not affect my grade, course completion, access to course activities, or relationship with the teacher. I understand that my responses may be used for research, analysis, and publication only in aggregated or anonymized form, so that I cannot be identified. I also understand that no special categories of personal data will be collected, and that I should avoid including sensitive personal information in free-text answers. The data will be handled confidentially and in accordance with GDPR. I may withdraw my consent before the data has been anonymized by contacting the responsible researcher.
> 
> Full information about the study, data handling, voluntariness, and withdrawal is available here.
> 
> Do you consent to your questionnaire responses being used for research purposes?

Response options: *Yes, I consent.* / *No, I do not consent.*

## Background items

| ID | Item | Response format |
|---|---|---|
| PRE-G | Select your seminar group number. | Group code (D1-G1 … D9-Gn) |
| PRE-AI | How often do you use AI for academic work? | Never / Occasionally / Sometimes / Often / Always |

## Likert items

5-point Likert scale from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).

| ID | Item |
|---|---|
| PRE-1 | I feel confident in my ability to explain the key ideas from the paper. |
| PRE-2 | I can critically evaluate the paper’s arguments, methods, or findings. |
| PRE-3 | I can support my explanations with evidence or reasoning from the paper. |
| PRE-4 | I feel prepared to engage actively in a seminar where I need to explain or defend my understanding. |
| PRE-5 | Our group has a shared understanding of the papers we have read. |
| PRE-6 | I am aware of which aspects of the paper I understand well and which aspects I find difficult. |

Note: the form also asked for the student's name, used only to match pre- and post-responses before anonymisation. Names are not published.

# Item mapping used in the pre–post analysis

This table documents which pre- and post-seminar items were analysed as pairs in the paper *Tutor or Peer?* and whether their wording is identical in content. Readers reusing the instrument should note that several pairs differ in wording or focus.

| Construct label (paper) | Pre item | Post item | Wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confidence explaining key ideas | PRE-1 | POST-1 | Same content (tense differs) |
| Critical evaluation | PRE-2 | POST-2 | Differs: self-rated ability (pre) vs. AI interaction encouraging evaluation (post) |
| Evidence-based answers | PRE-3 | POST-3 | Same content (tense differs) |
| Active engagement / preparedness | PRE-4 | POST-5 | Differs: preparedness to engage (pre) vs. felt actively engaged (post) |
| Group shared understanding | PRE-5 | POST-4 | Differs: shared understanding (pre) vs. worked well together (post) |
| Awareness of unclear aspects | PRE-6 | POST-8T / POST-8S | Differs: general awareness (pre) vs. AI interaction helped identify gaps (post); post wording is condition-specific |

Items POST-6, POST-7T/S and POST-9 were analysed cross-sectionally only.

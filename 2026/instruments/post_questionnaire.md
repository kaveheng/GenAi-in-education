# Post-seminar questionnaire

Administered individually immediately **after** the chatbot's final evaluation and before the group submitted the shared chat link.

## Background items

| ID | Item | Response format |
|---|---|---|
| POST-G | Select your group number. | Group code |
| POST-R | AI role | Tutor / Student |

## Likert items

5-point Likert scale from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).

| ID | Item | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| POST-1 | I felt confident explaining the key ideas from the paper during the session. | Both |
| POST-2 | The AI interaction encouraged me to critically evaluate the paper’s arguments, methods, or findings. | Both |
| POST-3 | I was able to support my answers with evidence or reasoning from the paper. | Both |
| POST-4 | Our group worked well together during the AI interaction. | Both |
| POST-5 | I felt actively engaged throughout the session. | Both |
| POST-6 | The AI interaction helped deepen my understanding of the papers. | Both |
| POST-7T | The AI’s questions challenged my group’s thinking about the paper. | AI Tutor |
| POST-7S | Identifying and correcting the AI’s misunderstandings challenged my group’s thinking about the paper. | AI Student |
| POST-8T | Being questioned by the AI helped me identify aspects of the paper that I had not fully understood before. | AI Tutor |
| POST-8S | Correcting the AI helped me identify aspects of the paper that I had not fully understood before. | AI Student |
| POST-9 | I was satisfied with the AI interaction as a way to reflect on and evaluate my understanding of the paper. | Both |

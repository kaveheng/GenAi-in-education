Grade-band assignment for all 36 groups, by evaluator

Interactive version of Fig. 1 in “Can AI Grade Dialogic Assessment? Human Agreement, Score Severity, and Run-to-Run Reliability” (K. Amouzgar, O. Mirmotahari, A. Lidfors Lindqvist, M. Urenda Moris). Each column is one student group, identified only by its rank on the human average; each cell shows the percentage score and its grade band. Post-seminar AI models are shown by their 10-run means.

U (<60%) Grade 3 (60–74%) Grade 4 (75–84%) Grade 5 (≥85%) S / T = AI Student / AI Tutor role · dark line = grade-band change in the human average
